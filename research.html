f. 3v

Green House Tower

Vertical greenhouse

Ten storeys of cultivation on circular tiers around a central lattice mast, with irrigation fed by gravity from a tank at the top.

Growing upwards instead of outwards frees farmland, which is returned to forest.

Published on the European Commission's InvestEU portal, reference INPO 20222430.

Estimated: 56 times the crop output per hectare of field farming, pending prototype measurement.

f. 4r

Martina & Parra Sustainable Communities Initiative

MP-SCI brings the group's technologies together in one self-sufficient settlement for a minimum of 100 families, generating an estimated 250 jobs.

A town inside a forest

The settlement is laid out like a Renaissance ideal town: a central square with a fountain, a ring of shared services, and 250 homes in garden crescents divided by six tree-lined boulevards.

Around the homes, a working ring holds the vertical greenhouses, orchard, community gardens, park, water and energy.

Because vertical cultivation needs a fraction of the land of field farming, 56 ha are returned to native forest around the town and along the river, crossed by walking trails.
