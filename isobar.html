ISOBAR

A closed hydraulic process for the generation of electrical energy from pressure differentials

1–3 MW per module

Design target, subject to engineering validation and a physical proof of concept.

ISOBAR guides water through a closed conduit shaped to create and use differences in pressure. The controlled movement of the water drives a turbine and generator.

Modules are designed to be installed alone or in groups, where site conditions and permits allow.

f. 9v

What it is designed to offer

Modular
Repeatable units, so a project can start small and grow unit by unit
Local
Generation near the point of use, for utilities, industrial sites, ports and islands
Predictable
Long-term energy contracts at a fixed price, structured under the applicable regulation

These are design aims. They become claims only once measured and independently verified.
