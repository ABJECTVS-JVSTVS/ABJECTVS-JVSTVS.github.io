The group

Martina & Parra develops its own technologies for energy, water and food, and turns them into infrastructure that can be manufactured, installed and repeated. We do not offer general consultancy: our engineering work serves ISOBAR, HYGROSTREAM and our research projects.

Leadership

Invention and executive management are kept apart. The founder leads the technology; the chief executive runs the company day to day: contracts, partnerships, finance and teams.

Ing. Dan Capriles Parra REnvP MSOE

Founder and Chief Inventor

Agroindustrial engineer, Universidad de San Buenaventura, Cali (2004). He began as a project engineer with rural indigenous communities in Colombia, developing sustainable-development plans with local leaders, then worked in Portugal before settling in the United Kingdom in 2018. He leads the conception and technical architecture of ISOBAR, HYGROSTREAM and the Green House Tower.

  • Registered Environmental Practitioner (REnvP)
  • Society of Operations Engineers, Member (MSOE)
  • Ordem dos Engenheiros, Região Sul: Membro Efetivo, Engenharia Agronómica
  • Sociedad Colombiana de Ingenieros, Member

David Martina Parra

Director, Martina & Parra Limited · Chief Executive Officer designate, Martina & Parra OÜ

Business Administrator, Universidad de San Buenaventura, Cali (2000), with more than twenty-five years in commercial management, financial services, executive advisory and project coordination.

He has advised chief executives and a vice-presidency, managed commercial units in insurance and financial planning, and coordinated projects for public, private and mixed-sector clients, including the logistics of Cali's annual Christmas lighting programme. His strengths are negotiation with government bodies, financial planning and leading teams.

He will lead Martina & Parra OÜ from its incorporation: administration, contracts, finance, institutional relations and European market development.

  • Professional licence in Business Administration, 2025
  • Languages: Spanish, English and Papiamento
